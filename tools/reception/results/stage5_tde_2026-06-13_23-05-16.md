6. **Iteration**: Steps 2-5 are repeated for multiple iterations (epochs) over the training dataset until the network’s performance stabilizes and converges to a satisfactory level of accuracy.

This learning process allows neural networks to automatically discover features from raw data, making them powerful tools for tasks like image recognition, natural language processing, and predictive modeling.
